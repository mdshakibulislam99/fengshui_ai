Yin and Yang Balance

Every room needs both calm (yin) and active (yang) energy. Too much of one creates stress or dullness.

  • Use softer light and warm fabrics in rest areas.
  • Use brighter light and open space where you work.
  • Avoid extreme contrast in one room.

Five Elements in Home Design

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water represent different qualities. Good Feng Shui uses all five in healthy proportion.

  • Wood: plants, vertical shapes, green tones.
  • Fire: warm lights, candles, red accents.
  • Earth: ceramics, square forms, beige tones.
  • Metal: white, gray, round forms, clean surfaces.
  • Water: mirrors, dark blue or black accents, curves.

Bagua Map Basics

The Bagua divides your space into life themes like career, health, relationships, and wealth.

  • Stand at your entrance and map 9 equal zones.
  • Choose one life area to improve first.
  • Add one intentional object linked to that goal.

Improve Qi Flow

Qi should move smoothly through the home. Blocked paths and clutter create stagnant energy.

  • Keep doors and walkways clear.
  • Repair broken items quickly.
  • Do a weekly 15-minute declutter session.

Bedroom Feng Shui Essentials

Your bedroom should support recovery, emotional stability, and safe sleep.

  • Place your bed where you can see the door but not align directly with it.
  • Use pairs (lamps, side tables) for relationship balance.
  • Limit mirrors directly facing the bed.

Work Desk Command Position

Your desk placement influences confidence and focus.

  • Sit with a solid wall behind you when possible.
  • Face the room entrance if possible.
  • Keep your most-used tools on your supportive side.

7-Day Beginner Practice Plan

  • Day 1: Remove obvious clutter at your entrance.
  • Day 2: Improve light quality in one room.
  • Day 3: Add one healthy plant to activate wood energy.
  • Day 4: Reposition your desk or chair for support.
  • Day 5: Balance colors in your bedroom for calm.
  • Day 6: Map one Bagua area and place one goal object.
  • Day 7: Review what changed in your mood and focus.
